### 📞 [GTM Performance: Outreach Efficiency & Account Prioritization](https://app.hex.tech/019e4d33-0ab5-7000-8cc1-1feb8c334cb6/app/GTM-Performance-Outreach-Efficiency-Account-Prioritization-033ZCobLrURfRZH1UJJl8u/latest)
**Hex · SQL · Python · Monte Carlo Simulation · GTM Analytics · CRM Analytics**

End-to-end GTM performance analysis for an outpatient telehealth company's clinical outreach team, covering Feb 2023–Jan 2024. Tracked 13,834 logged activities (6,853 calls, 6,981 meetings) against 6,824 L12M referrals, surfacing a +62.2% YoY referral growth alongside a concerning H1→H2 decline of 13.1% as activity volume grew 11.4% — revealing a referral yield problem, not a volume problem. Referral yield collapsed from 85–90 referrals per 100 activities in early 2023 to 21.5 in December, driven by a call-heavy strategy that produced no referral uplift. Account concentration analysis found the top 10% of accounts (D10) responsible for 64.9% of all referrals, informing a tiered account prioritization framework. Rep-level scatter analysis exposed wide performance variance independent of activity volume, with attribution gaps from unlogged activities distorting coaching signals. Concluded with Monte Carlo simulations (10,000 runs) modeling the impact of CRM tooling improvements (Dialpad/RingCentral integration): projecting a median of 862 incremental annual activities and 188 incremental referrals by recovering 6–12% of rep time lost to logging friction — with an ∼80% probability of exceeding 150 net-new referrals from the initiative alone.
